This policy explains how Metin Akanay (“we”, “us”) handles information in Velora: Arrow Puzzle and on this website.

At a glance

Your journey can be played offline. Game progress is saved on your device. If you join the leaderboard, your chosen nickname and ranking information are visible to other players. Advertising services process information when ads are enabled and requested.

You do not need to provide a real name, email address, or password to play. Please do not put personal or sensitive information in your leaderboard nickname.

1. Information we handle

On your device

The game saves progress, best stars for levels, daily challenge status, coins, helper items, unlocked themes, preferences, and tutorial status. It also stores authentication state, pending progress updates, and cached leaderboard or version information needed for the relevant features.

Online rankings

When you use the leaderboard, Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous player identifier. Firebase Cloud Firestore stores your identifier, chosen nickname, score, journey progress, daily challenge completions, and completion records with synchronization timestamps. Nicknames, scores, and ranks are displayed to other players. “Anonymous” means no email or password is requested; the identifier still distinguishes your player profile.

Service and advertising information

Our hosting, backend, and advertising providers may process IP addresses, device and app information, diagnostic or security logs, and request timestamps to operate their services. Advertising may also involve advertising identifiers, approximate location inferred from an IP address, ad interactions, and consent signals, depending on platform permissions and provider settings.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include, and use it to respond. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

The game does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location. We do not integrate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics in the current version.

2. Why we use information

  • Save your progress and preferences, including while offline.
  • Synchronize completed levels and stars, calculate rankings, and avoid crediting the same completion repeatedly.
  • Provide daily challenges and check supported app versions.
  • Deliver ads and optional video rewards when available, manage consent, and help prevent abuse.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests, maintain security, and meet applicable obligations.

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the service you request, legitimate interests such as security and support, legal obligations, or consent where required. You may contact us about these grounds or object to processing based on legitimate interests.

3. Advertising and rewards

Advertising-enabled versions use Google AdMob for banner ads and optional rewarded videos. The current implementation requests non-personalized ads. Non-personalized advertising can still process information for ad delivery, frequency limits, measurement, security, and fraud prevention; it does not mean no data is processed.

When required, Google’s consent flow asks for privacy choices before ads are requested. Device permissions and the consent choices available in your region affect processing. You can also manage advertising permissions in your device settings and contact us about withdrawing consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Rewarded videos are optional. A reward is granted only after the advertising service confirms it was earned. Ads and video rewards require connectivity and availability. Journey gameplay remains available offline.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising information.

4. Service providers and sharing

  • Google Firebase: anonymous authentication, leaderboard records, and app configuration. See Firebase privacy and security.
  • Google AdMob and its advertising partners: advertising and consent processing when ads are enabled. Applicable providers and choices may be shown in the consent flow.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting this website. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

We do not sell your leaderboard profile or support messages. We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, security, and legal rights. Providers may process information outside your country, under their applicable contractual and legal safeguards.

5. Storage, retention, and security

Local progress remains until it is reset or removed from your device; operating system backups may retain a copy. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete records already synchronized to Firebase.

We retain online player and completion records while needed to provide your ranking and prevent duplicate credit, unless you request deletion. Support messages are retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet legal or security needs. Provider logs and backups follow the providers’ retention schedules. We do not promise immediate deletion of independent provider backups or records that must legally be retained.

We use access controls and encrypted network connections supported by our providers. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and deletion requests

You can play Journey offline, choose not to join the leaderboard, and decline optional rewarded videos. Offline progress may synchronize when you reconnect if you have enabled leaderboard participation.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your player profile and associated completion records, email metinakanay@gmail.com with the subject “Velora privacy request”. Include your nickname and, if available, your player identifier. Do not post this information publicly. We may need additional information to verify that the profile belongs to you, especially because profiles do not have email logins.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, and a complaint to your local data protection authority. We respond within the time required by applicable law. Removing the app or clearing local data alone is not a request to delete online records; contact us before removing information that may help identify your profile.

7. Children’s privacy

Velora is intended for a mixed audience, including children under 13. The game does not ask players for a real name, email address, or password. A nickname can nevertheless reveal personal information; parents and guardians should help children avoid using identifying details. The data categories and providers described above are relevant to online features and advertising.

Pre-release notice: The mobile game has not yet launched. The child-facing online features and advertising settings are still being finalized and must be reviewed before release. This policy does not represent that age screening, parental consent, or child-specific advertising controls are already implemented.

Parents and guardians may contact us to ask about a child's information, request access or deletion, or object to further processing. We may need proportionate information to verify the request. Please email metinakanay@gmail.com privately rather than posting a child's details publicly.

8. This website

The interactive puzzle and theme selector run in your browser. This website does not add analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, or external fonts. Demo progress and theme choices are held in memory and reset when the page reloads. Your language preference is saved in your browser's local storage. GitHub Pages may process technical access information, including IP addresses, to host and secure the site.

External links take you to services with their own policies.

9. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when features or data practices change. The updated policy and date will appear on this page; additional notice or consent will be provided where required.
